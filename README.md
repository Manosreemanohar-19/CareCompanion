<p align="center">
LearnEase is an AI-powered learning chatbot that acts like a personal tutor and friend.  
It delivers customized learning paths, topic explanations, and quizzes — all through interactive conversations.  
Designed to assist students in self-paced study, LearnEase merges NLP, ML, and EdTech to revolutionize learning. 🌟

<h2 align="center">🎯 Features</h2>

- 🤖 AI chatbot for personalized topic-wise assistance.
- 📚 Covers subjects like Python, Math, DS, AI/ML basics, and more.
- 🧪 Topic-based quizzes with instant feedback.
- 🧩 NLP-based conversational engine for human-like interaction.
- 🎯 Tailored recommendations based on student’s performance.
- 🌐 Deployed as a simple and accessible website.
